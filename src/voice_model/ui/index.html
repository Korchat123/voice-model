Step 02

Add a clean voice reference

Optional for synthetic voices; required when adapting a real voice.

01 Find a quiet room

Turn off fans, music, notifications, and noisy appliances.

02 Keep one position

Stay 15–20 cm from the microphone and avoid touching it.

03 Speak naturally

Use a comfortable pace and energy. Do not force a character.

04 Check every take

Reject clipping, echoes, interruptions, and incorrect words.
